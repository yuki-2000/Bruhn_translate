## A3.9 Product of Inertia for Axes of Symmetry.



If an area is symmetrical about two rectangular axes, the product of inertia about these
axes is zero. This follows from the fact that
symmetrical axes are centroidal x and y axes.
If an area is symmetrical about only one of
two rectangular axes, the product of inertia,
$\int xy dA$, is zero because for each product $ xy dA $ for
an element on one side of the axis of symmetry,
there is an equal product of opposite sign for
the corresponding element dA on the opposite side
of the axis, thus making the expression $\int y dA$
equal to zero.
